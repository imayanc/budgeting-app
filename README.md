# Simple Budget App

A clean, modern budgeting application to track your income and expenses.

## Features

- 💰 **Track Income & Expenses** - Easily add transactions with descriptions and categories
- 📊 **Live Summary** - See your total income, expenses, and remaining balance at a glance
- 📋 **Transaction History** - View all your transactions with dates and categories
- 💾 **Local Storage** - Your data is saved automatically in your browser
- 📱 **Responsive Design** - Works great on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- 🎨 **Modern UI** - Beautiful gradient design with smooth animations

## Getting Started

1. Open `index.html` in a web browser
2. Add your first transaction:
   - Enter a description (e.g., "Salary", "Groceries")
   - Enter the amount
   - Select the type (Income or Expense)
   - Choose a category
   - Click "Add Transaction"

## How It Works

- **Income** - Money coming in (salary, bonuses, investments, etc.)
- **Expenses** - Money going out (food, transport, entertainment, etc.)
- **Balance** - Income minus expenses (your remaining money)

The app automatically:
- Updates the summary totals
- Saves your transactions to your browser's local storage
- Displays all transactions in reverse chronological order

## Data

Your data is stored locally in your browser and persists between sessions. Clearing your browser's cache/cookies will delete your data.

## Tips

- Use descriptive names for transactions (e.g., "Grocery shopping" instead of just "Grocery")
- Regularly review your expenses to identify spending patterns
- Set budget goals and track towards them
- Delete transactions if you make a mistake
